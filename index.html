How to play

Steer the snake with arrow keys / WASD (or swipe).
Eat letters to add them to your body, in order.
When the most recent letters you ate spell a crossword answer, the word locks into the puzzle and those letters fall off.
Hit a wall or yourself and it's over. Fill the whole crossword to win!
